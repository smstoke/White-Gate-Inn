Frequently asked questions

Planning Your Stay

Everything guests ask before booking. Can’t find an answer? Get in touch and we’ll help.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A complimentary traditional breakfast with pastries and buffet-style favorites is served each morning.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and check-out is at 11:00 AM.

Is parking available?

Yes. Complimentary off-street parking is available for all guests.

Can I walk to downtown and the lake?

Yes. The inn is in the heart of downtown Spirit Lake, within walking distance of restaurants, shops, parks, and the lake shoreline.

What rooms do you offer?

Four upstairs guest rooms and two main-floor suites, all inside the restored 1907 church.

How many guests can stay in a room or suite?

Each guest room sleeps a maximum of 2 guests. Each suite sleeps a maximum of 4 guests.

Do the rooms have private bathrooms and fireplaces?

Yes. Every guest room and suite has a private bathroom and a gas fireplace.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is included in every guest room and suite.

Do the suites have kitchens?

Yes. Each suite has a fully equipped kitchen, a separate bedroom with a queen bed, a living area, and a queen hide-a-bed. Suites also have air conditioning.

What is the history of the building?

The inn was built in 1907 as St. Joseph’s Catholic Church and served the Spirit Lake community for decades. Owner Steve Stoke purchased it in 2018 and restored it as a boutique bed and breakfast, preserving the original moldings, soaring ceilings, and architectural details.

What is there to do nearby?

Spirit Lake offers boating, kayaking, paddleboarding, swimming, fishing, and sunset walks, plus hiking, mountain biking, and scenic drives nearby. Silverwood Theme Park is a short drive away, and Coeur d’Alene is about 30 minutes away.
