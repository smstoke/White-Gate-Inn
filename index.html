Welcome

A historic church transformed into boutique lodging in the heart of Spirit Lake, Idaho.

Step inside a place where history, architecture, and hospitality come together. Originally built in 1907 as St. Joseph’s Catholic Church, our beautifully restored bed and breakfast offers a one-of-a-kind stay unlike anywhere else in North Idaho.

Thoughtfully renovated while preserving its original craftsmanship, each guest room and suite features soaring ceilings, restored woodwork, private bathrooms, cozy gas fireplaces, and modern comforts. Whether you’re planning a romantic getaway, a relaxing weekend at the lake, or exploring North Idaho, you’ll experience the warmth of boutique hospitality within a landmark that has welcomed generations.

More than a place to stay

A Piece of Spirit Lake History

Originally built in 1907 as St. Joseph’s Catholic Church, this landmark has been thoughtfully restored into a one-of-a-kind boutique bed and breakfast. Every detail of the renovation was completed with care to preserve the building’s remarkable craftsmanship—from the original wood moldings and soaring ceilings to the architectural character that has welcomed generations.

Today, guests experience the perfect blend of historic charm, modern comfort, and genuine hospitality in a place where the past has been beautifully renewed. Whether you’re visiting for a romantic getaway, a weekend at the lake, or simply looking for unique lodging in North Idaho, you’ll find a stay unlike any other.
