Planning Your Stay

View all FAQs
Is breakfast included?

Yes. A complimentary homemade breakfast is served buffet style each morning.

What time is check-in?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and check-out is at 11:00 AM.

Is parking available?

Yes. Complimentary off-street parking is available for all guests.

Can I walk to downtown and the lake?

Yes. The inn is in the heart of downtown Spirit Lake, within walking distance of restaurants, shops, parks, and the lake shoreline.
