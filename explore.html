Explore Spirit Lake, Idaho

Discover the Charm of North Idaho

Whether you’re planning a relaxing weekend getaway or an adventure-filled vacation, Spirit Lake offers the perfect balance of small-town charm and outdoor recreation. Staying at our historic bed and breakfast places you in the heart of downtown, just steps from local shops, restaurants, and the beautiful shoreline of Spirit Lake.

From peaceful mornings on the water to scenic mountain drives and nearby attractions, there’s something to enjoy in every season.

Stroll Through Historic Downtown

Our bed and breakfast is conveniently located in downtown Spirit Lake, where you can explore locally owned shops, cafés, restaurants, and community events. On the water

Spend the Day at Spirit Lake

Just a short walk from the inn, Spirit Lake is the centerpiece of our community. Surrounded by forests and mountains, the lake offers endless opportunities to relax and enjoy the outdoors.

Whether you’re soaking up the sun on the beach, launching a boat, casting a fishing line, or enjoying a peaceful paddle, Spirit Lake is a favorite destination for visitors throughout the year.

Popular lake activities include

  • Boating
  • Kayaking
  • Paddleboarding
  • Swimming
  • Fishing
  • Wildlife Viewing
  • Sunset Walks

The outdoors

Explore North Idaho’s Natural Beauty

The Spirit Lake area is surrounded by beautiful forests, lakes, and mountain scenery. Outdoor enthusiasts will find miles of hiking and biking trails, scenic drives, and countless places to reconnect with nature.

No matter the season, North Idaho offers breathtaking landscapes waiting to be explored.

Outdoor adventures include

  • Hiking
  • Mountain Biking
  • Scenic Drives
  • Bird Watching
  • Snowshoeing
  • Cross-Country Skiing
  • Photography

A short drive

Silverwood Theme Park

Looking for family fun? Silverwood Theme Park is just a short drive from Spirit Lake and features thrilling roller coasters, family rides, live entertainment, and Boulder Beach Water Park. It’s one of the Northwest’s most popular attractions and makes a perfect day trip during your stay.

About 30 minutes away

Visit Coeur d’Alene

Only about 30 minutes away, Coeur d’Alene offers even more to explore. Spend the day shopping downtown, dining at waterfront restaurants, taking a cruise on Lake Coeur d’Alene, or walking the famous floating boardwalk at the Coeur d’Alene Resort.

Many of our guests enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of Spirit Lake while taking advantage of everything nearby Coeur d’Alene has to offer.

Every season offers something special

Four Seasons in North Idaho

Spring

Watch North Idaho come to life with blooming wildflowers, comfortable temperatures, and quiet hiking trails.

Summer

Spend long days on the lake, enjoy local events, and experience warm evenings perfect for relaxing by the fireplace after sunset.

Fall

Crisp air and vibrant autumn colors make fall one of the most beautiful times to visit Spirit Lake.

Winter

Snow-covered landscapes create a peaceful retreat, with nearby opportunities for skiing, snowshoeing, and cozy evenings inside our historic inn.
