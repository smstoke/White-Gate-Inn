Suites

Spacious Historic Suites for Families & Extended Stays

Located on the main floor of the beautifully restored church, our two boutique suites offer additional space without sacrificing the character and charm that make your stay truly memorable.

Suite photography coming soon

Ideal for families, couples traveling together, or guests planning a longer visit, each suite includes a fully equipped kitchen, private bedroom, comfortable living area, and queen hide-a-bed for additional sleeping space.

Original architectural details, high ceilings, restored woodwork, and a cozy gas fireplace create an inviting retreat that celebrates the building’s rich history while providing all the conveniences of modern lodging.

After a day exploring Spirit Lake, Coeur d’Alene, or the surrounding mountains, return to a peaceful space where comfort and history meet.
