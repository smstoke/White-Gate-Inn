Guest Rooms

Historic Guest Rooms in Spirit Lake, Idaho

Designed for couples seeking a peaceful getaway, our four upstairs guest rooms blend the timeless beauty of a 1907 church with the comforts of modern boutique lodging.

Guest room photography coming soon

Each room has been thoughtfully restored to preserve the building’s original craftsmanship while providing everything you need for a relaxing stay. Original moldings, soaring ceilings, and architectural details create an atmosphere unlike any traditional hotel, while cozy gas fireplaces, private bathrooms, and comfortable queen beds ensure every guest feels at home.

Wake each morning to a complimentary traditional breakfast featuring pastries and buffet-style favorites before exploring downtown Spirit Lake, enjoying the lake, or discovering all that North Idaho has to offer.

Whether you’re celebrating an anniversary, enjoying a weekend getaway, or simply looking for a unique place to stay, our guest rooms offer an unforgettable experience where history, architecture, and hospitality come together.
